critical

important
Maintain CPAP with in-line nebulizer; do not break the mask seal
DO NOT discontinue CPAP/BiPAP until patient is on the emergency department stretcher and hospital CPAP/BiPAP is immediately available for patient to be switched over, or physician is at bedside and requesting CPAP/BiPAP be discontinued.
