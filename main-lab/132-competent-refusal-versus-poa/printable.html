↳ MATC: Agitated or Violent Patient/Behavioral Emergency
critical
Explain that POA activates only when the patient lacks capacity
If the patient lacks the capacity to make decisions regarding treatment and/or transport, identify any advanced care planning in place for information relating to advanced care planning and consent for treatment: Advance directives; Medical/Physician Order for Life-Sustaining Treatment (MOLST/POLST) or similar directing forms; Guardian, power of attorney, or other accepted healthcare proxy. -- As specified from state to state, in the absence of formal written directions (MOLST, POLST, DNR, advanced directives), and in the presence of a person with power of attorney for healthcare or healthcare proxy, that person may prescribe limits of treatment.

important

↳ MATC: End-of-Life Care/Hospice Care
critical
Continue symptom-directed treatment; a DNR is not a do-not-treat order
If there is a valid exclusion to resuscitation and there are signs of life (pulse and respirations), EMS clinicians should provide standard appropriate treatment under existing protocols according to the patient's condition. The patient should receive full treatment per protocols with the exception of any intervention specifically prohibited in the patient's valid exclusion to resuscitation.
