Improper Care Branch
Dextrose delayed; seizure occurs
Trigger: The blood glucose was never corrected, either because it was not checked or because a failed IV was not escalated to IO or IM glucagon. Profound hypoglycemia has produced a seizure with hypoventilation and hypoxia.
Eight to ten minutes into the call the glucose has still not been corrected: either it was never checked, or the IV failed and no alternative route was used. The patient has stopped responding to painful stimulus, her muscles begin to twitch, and she has a generalized tonic-clonic seizure lasting one to two minutes. Her respirations have fallen to 8 per minute and are irregular, her saturation has dropped to 86 percent, and the glucometer now reads LOW. This is neuroglycopenic injury in progress and requires immediate ventilation and dextrose.
