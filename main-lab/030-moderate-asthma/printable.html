Expected Actions
critical
Initiate CPAP with nebulizer administered in-line via T-connector
Non-invasive ventilation (NIV) should be administered for severe respiratory distress via BVM, continuous positive airway pressure (CPAP) or bi-level positive airway pressure (BiPAP). If indicated, bronchodilators should be administered in line with NIV. Nebulized medications may be administered through a CPAP or BiPAP mask. A specialized T-connector with a spring valve assembly is required to allow maintenance of positive airway pressure
↳ MATC: Airway Management (Includes RSI)
critical
Maintain continuous CPAP mask seal; do not discontinue en route
Once initiated and patient is tolerating mask, DO NOT discontinue CPAP/BiPAP until patient is on the emergency department stretcher and hospital CPAP/BiPAP is immediately available for patient to be switched over, or physician is at bedside and requesting CPAP/BiPAP be discontinued. Breaking the mask seal causes a significant decrease in airway pressures and may lead to abrupt decompensation due to atelectasis and alveolar collapse
