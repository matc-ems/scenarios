S Signs & Symptoms
Home health aide arrived for a scheduled visit and found the patient seated in tripod position at the kitchen table with audible expiratory wheezing and pursed-lip breathing. The aide reports the patient's oxygen concentrator was unplugged and his nasal cannula was off. She also observed the patient using his rescue inhaler incorrectly earlier in the week — not exhaling fully before actuating and no breath-hold afterward — and states he has missed both morning and evening doses of his maintenance inhaler for the past 2 days after running low on refills. Patient reports gradually worsening shortness of breath since waking this morning, worse with any exertion, denies chest pain, fever, or recent illness.
