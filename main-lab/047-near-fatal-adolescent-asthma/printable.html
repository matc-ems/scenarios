Expected Actions
critical
Continuous albuterol 5 mg + ipratropium 0.5 mg nebulized, repeated with unlimited frequency for ongoing distress
Albuterol 5 mg nebulized (or 6 puffs metered dose inhaler) should be administered to all patients in respiratory distress with signs of bronchospasm. Repeat at this dose with unlimited frequency for ongoing respiratory distress. Ipratropium 0.5 mg nebulized should be given up to 3 doses in conjunction with albuterol
↳ MATC: Respiratory Distress (includes Bronchospasm, Pulmonary Edema)
critical
Epinephrine 0.3 mg IM (1 mg/mL) (55 kg × 0.01 mg/kg = 0.55 mg → capped at max 0.3 mg); adjunctive for impending respiratory failure
Epinephrine (0.01 mg/kg of 1 mg/mL solution IM, maximum dose of 0.3 mg) should only be administered for impending respiratory failure as adjunctive therapy when there are no clinical signs of improvement with the above treatments
↳ MATC: Respiratory Distress (includes Bronchospasm, Pulmonary Edema)
critical
Magnesium sulfate 2 g IV (55 kg × 40 mg/kg = 2200 mg → capped at max 2 g) over 10-15 minutes
Magnesium sulfate (40 mg/kg IV, maximum dose of 2 g) over 10-15 minutes should be administered for severe bronchoconstriction and concern for impending respiratory failure
↳ MATC: Respiratory Distress (includes Bronchospasm, Pulmonary Edema)
