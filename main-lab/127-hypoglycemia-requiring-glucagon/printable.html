↳ MATC: Hypoglycemia
critical

If blood glucose is 60 mg/dL or less administer one of the following: Conscious patient with a patent airway: Glucose, oral (in form of glucose tablets, glucose gel, tube of cake icing, etc.). Unconscious patient, or patients who are unable to protect their own airway: Dextrose IV.

critical

↳ MATC: Hypoglycemia
BGL checked; glucagon 1 mg IM given
Trigger: Intramuscular glucagon mobilizes hepatic glycogen stores; serum glucose rises over 10 to 15 minutes and mental status follows, with resolution of the sympathetic response that produced the tachycardia and diaphoresis.
Within several minutes of the intramuscular glucagon the patient begins tracking the providers and can state his name. Over roughly fifteen minutes he becomes alert and oriented to person, place, time, and event. His skin turns pink, warm, and dry, his heart rate falls, and a repeat glucose is 110 mg/dL. He reports feeling shaky and does not remember the last hour.
