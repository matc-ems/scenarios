Expected Actions
critical
Initiate CPAP for severe pulmonary edema, before or simultaneous with medication therapy
Restoration of adequate oxygenation and ventilation should precede or be accomplished simultaneously with other medication therapies below. CPAP/BiPAP: See Airway Management Guideline for goals of care and escalation of interventions
↳ MATC: Respiratory Distress (includes Bronchospasm, Pulmonary Edema)
critical
Screen for phosphodiesterase-5 inhibitor use (sildenafil, vardenafil, tadalafil) within the past 48 hours before giving nitrates
The use of nitrates should be avoided in any patient who has used a phosphodiesterase inhibitor within the past 48 hours. Examples are sildenafil, vardenafil and tadalafil, which are used for erectile dysfunction and pulmonary hypertension
↳ MATC: Respiratory Distress (includes Bronchospasm, Pulmonary Edema)
