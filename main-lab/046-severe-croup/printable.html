Expected Actions
critical
Nebulized epinephrine 5 mg (5 mL of 1 mg/mL solution); may repeat in 20 minutes
Inhaled medications should be administered to all children with croup in respiratory distress with signs of stridor at rest—these medications should be repeated at this dose with unlimited frequency for ongoing respiratory distress. Epinephrine 5 mg (5 mL of 1 mg/mL solution) nebulized (may repeat in 20 minutes as needed)
