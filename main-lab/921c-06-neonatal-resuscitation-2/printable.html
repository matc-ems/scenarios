Expected Actions
critical
Provide aggressive warming: dry, swaddle, warm blankets covering body and head
Newborn infants are prone to hypothermia which may lead to hypoglycemia, hypoxia, and lethargy. Aggressive warming techniques should be initiated including drying, swaddling, and warm blankets covering body and head. When available, radiant warmers or other warming adjuncts are suggested for babies who require resuscitation, especially for preterm babies

critical
Initiate BVM ventilation with 100% oxygen at 40-60 breaths/min for 90 seconds
Positive pressure ventilation (PPV) with bag-mask device may be initiated with room air (21% oxygen) in term and late preterm babies; otherwise use 100% oxygen. Initiate BVM ventilation with room air at 40–60 breaths per minute for 90 seconds

critical
