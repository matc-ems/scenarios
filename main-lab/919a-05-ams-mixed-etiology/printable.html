Expected Actions
critical
Recognize glipizide's long half-life; transport despite improvement (recurrent hypoglycemia risk)
Sulfonylureas (e.g., glyburide, glipizide) have long half-lives ranging from 12–60 hrs. Patients with corrected hypoglycemia who are taking these agents are at particular risk for recurrent symptoms and frequently require hospital admission

important
Recognize fever as a disqualifying co-morbid symptom; do not consider release, transport
If symptoms of hypoglycemia resolve after treatment, release without transport should only be considered if all the following are true: ... No major co-morbid symptoms exist, like chest pain, shortness of breath, seizures, intoxication
