Expected Actions
critical
Support head with gentle counterpressure to control delivery pace
Delivery should be controlled to allow a slow controlled delivery of infant – This will prevent injury to mother. Support the infant's head as needed and apply gentle counterpressure to help prevent the head from suddenly popping out
↳ MATC: OB/GYN Childbirth
critical
Check for nuchal cord after head delivers; slip over head if present
Check for nuchal cord (i.e., around the baby's neck) If present, slip it over the head. If unable to free the cord from the neck, double clamp the cord and cut between the clamps
↳ MATC: OB/GYN Childbirth
critical
Guide head down to deliver anterior shoulder, then up for posterior shoulder
Grasping the head with hand over the ears, gently guide head down to allow delivery of the anterior shoulder. Gently guide the head up to allow delivery of the posterior shoulder. Slowly deliver the remainder of the infant
↳ MATC: OB/GYN Childbirth
important
Do not routinely suction airway during delivery, even with bulb syringe
Do not routinely suction the infant's airway (even with a bulb syringe) during delivery
↳ MATC: OB/GYN Childbirth
Newborn dried, warmed, APGAR assessed
Trigger: Following an uncomplicated shoulder and body delivery, the newborn cries vigorously with good tone and color, prompting routine care rather than resuscitation.
The newborn is dried, warmed, and stimulated on the maternal chest with a strong cry and good tone. APGAR is scored at 1 and 5 minutes while the cord is clamped and cut and the placenta is allowed to deliver spontaneously.
